# 03 — Problem Framing

## The problem

Entrepreneurs and small business owners track income and expenses in
spreadsheets or paper notebooks updated inconsistently. Nobody can answer
"what is my balance right now" without manually adding up scattered entries,
and an entry deleted or miscategorized by mistake cannot be traced back.

## Who is affected

| Group | How |
|---|---|
| Business owner / entrepreneur | Cannot tell today's balance without manually totaling multiple sheets or notebook pages |
| Small business staff | Enter transactions inconsistently across copies, unaware which one is current |

## Cost of doing nothing

A miscategorized or accidentally lost transaction costs staff time to
reconstruct from memory or a paper trail, and an owner who cannot see today's
balance risks spending money the business does not actually have on hand.

## Success criterion

The problem is solved when a business owner can see the current balance and
the most recent transactions in one view, every transaction is safely
recoverable if deleted by mistake, and no manual addition across separate
sheets is ever required.
